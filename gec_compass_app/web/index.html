GECT Compass (GEC Maps & GECT Maps) - GEC Thrissur Campus Navigation

Indoor and outdoor map application for Government Engineering College (GEC) Thrissur. Locate departments, labs, classrooms, hostels, auditoriums, and campus amenities with real-time GPS & offline GEC Navigator routing.

Key Keywords & Features - GECT Compass, GEC Maps & GECT Maps

  • GEC Maps: Interactive high-definition indoor and outdoor map of GEC Thrissur campus.
  • GECT Compass: Live compass heading and real-time turn-by-turn navigation across college blocks.
  • GEC Navigator: Offline-capable routing system for offline campus navigation.
  • GECT Maps: Complete directory of departments, computer labs, workshops, and facilities at GEC Thrissur.
