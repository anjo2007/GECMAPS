GECT Compass | GECT Maps & GEC Maps – GEC Thrissur Campus Navigation System

Welcome to GECT Compass (also known as GECT Maps, GEC Maps, GEC Compass, or gect compasss) — the official digital campus map, indoor room finder, and turn-by-turn navigation system for Government Engineering College (GEC) Thrissur, Kerala, India.

About GECT Compass & GECT Maps

GECT Compass provides interactive indoor and outdoor spatial navigation across the entire 120-acre campus of Government Engineering College Thrissur. Whether you are a student, faculty member, parent, or campus visitor searching for "GECT Maps", "GEC Maps", "GECT Compass", or "GEC Navigator", GECT Compass helps you quickly find every department block, laboratory, seminar hall, administrative office, hostel, and canteen with real-time GPS location tracking, hardware compass heading, and Dijkstra shortest-path walking routes.

Key Campus Navigation Features & Capabilities

  • GECT Maps & GEC Maps: Comprehensive vector and satellite mapping covering all college buildings, pedestrian walkways, gates, and roads.
  • GECT Compass Direction: Real-time device compass orientation and live blue-dot GPS tracking to guide your walk across campus.
  • GEC Navigator Turn-by-Turn Routing: Dijkstra graph-based shortest road and walkway paths directly to your destination without unnecessary gate detours.
  • Indoor Room & Lab Finder: Detailed floor-wise search for lecture halls, laboratories, faculty rooms, and department seminar halls.
  • Offline Map Support: Local caching technology allowing smooth campus navigation even in low or zero mobile data connectivity zones.
  • Cross-Platform Accessibility: Accessible instantly online as a Progressive Web App (PWA) and available as a lightweight native Android APK.
